# Funding channels for Buyer Arena (use only if the pre-launch audit passes)

Research date: 2026-09-25/26. Status: research only. Nothing has been submitted and no one has been contacted.

**Founder profile used for eligibility:** Spanish national living in Arnhem (Gelderland, NL). Solo founder, no legal entity yet (options: Dutch BV or eenmanszaak, or Spanish SL). Apache-2.0 open core. Pre-launch RC, no users, no revenue.

## How this was verified (read first)

- **Method.** The sandbox egress proxy blocked direct page fetches (WebFetch/curl) for nearly every
  funder domain (nlnet.nl, sovereign.tech, rvo.nl, eic.ec.europa.eu, ycombinator.com, …). The only
  full pages I could read were on **github.com**. Everything else comes from **search-engine
  excerpts of the official page at the cited URL**, read on 2026-09-25/26. Re-open each URL before
  you apply.
- **Labels.**
  - `OK`: excerpt comes from the official domain cited.
  - `2nd`: only a secondary source (press, consultant, aggregator) said it. Treat it as a lead.
  - `UNVERIFIED`: I could not confirm it, or it is my inference. It is flagged inline.
- **Fit score (1–5).** 5 = apply as soon as launch prerequisites are met. 1 = not worth time at this stage.
- **"Next deadline"** is the next one after 2026-09-26 that I could find. "Rolling" = no fixed cut-off.

## What the pitch must prove

| Channel type                                                            | Must prove                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Leave out                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **OSS funds** (NLnet, Sovereign Tech, GitHub SOSF, Alpha-Omega)         | Public-interest value of the _open_ deliverable (portable evidence format, reproducibility, privacy: "what left your machine"). Concrete, verifiable milestones with an hours × rate budget. The licence stays OSI-approved. Comparison with existing tools and why yours is not a duplicate. How the commons survives if the company fails. For STF/Alpha-Omega specifically: that others _depend_ on it                                                                                    | SaaS revenue plans as the main story. Open-core features that would be withheld from the funded work                      |
| **Public grants and loans** (WBSO, MIT, Innovatiekrediet, EIC, Horizon) | **Technical novelty and technical risk** (WBSO/Innovatiekrediet reject commercial risk). Measurable technical objectives. NL/EU establishment. For EIC: a breakthrough claim, a large EU market, a scale-up plan, customer LOIs, freedom to operate, team. For Horizon: the work package you own inside a consortium                                                                                                                                                                         | Pure "we'll build features" roadmaps. Anything already built before the application date (WBSO/VFF only fund future work) |
| **Accelerators and VCs**                                                | Pain and urgency: teams shipping AI-agent code need evidence before merging or releasing, and current evals are fragmented. **Why now** (agentic coding, AI Act evidence duties). Early pull: users, design partners, stars/dependents trend, one case study. Open-core monetisation (hosted runs, team dashboards, enterprise policy and audit trails), with the free core as a distribution engine. Founder speed (RC shipped solo). Market sizing across QA, security and AI-eval budgets | Claims you cannot demo live. "No competitors". Revenue projections without a pricing hypothesis                           |
